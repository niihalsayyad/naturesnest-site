Amenities

Made for slow, beautiful days

From the pool to the spa, every space at Nature's Nest is designed around nature, comfort and calm.

Home  /  Amenities
01 Dive into calm

Swimming Pool

Our sparkling outdoor pool is framed by palm trees, sun loungers and shady umbrellas, with the green hills rising all around. Spend lazy afternoons by the water or take a sunset swim as the sky turns gold.

  • Sun loungers & umbrellas
  • Palm-fringed poolside
  • Hill & sea views
  • Fresh towels provided
02 Restore body and mind

Spa & Massage

Step into our tranquil spa, where warm lighting, natural textures and the rustle of the garden set the mood. Unwind with a soothing massage and let the stress of the city melt away.

  • Relaxing massage therapies
  • Calm, garden-facing rooms
  • Ideal for couples
  • Prior booking recommended
03 Dining with a view

Restaurant

Our open-air restaurant brings together wholesome food, rattan furniture and a gentle sea breeze. Enjoy relaxed breakfasts and candle-lit dinners with the palms and ocean as your backdrop.

  • Open-air seating
  • Views of palms & sea
  • Family-friendly
  • Perfect for celebrations
04 Sunset sips

Bar & Lounge

With a leafy ceiling, glowing rattan lamps and wide views of the coastline, our lounge is the place to gather at golden hour for drinks, conversation and good company.

  • Relaxed lounge seating
  • Sunset views
  • Refreshing beverages
  • Warm, cosy ambience
05 Stay active

Fitness Centre

Keep up your routine in our bright, modern gym — complete with treadmills, cross-trainers and free weights, all facing floor-to-ceiling windows onto the forest.

  • Cardio machines
  • Free weights & benches
  • Forest views
  • Air-conditioned
06 Fun for little ones

Kids' Play Area

Children are always welcome at Nature's Nest. Our colourful outdoor play area — with slides, swings and soft green lawns — keeps young explorers happy while parents relax nearby.

  • Slides & swings
  • Soft lawns & gardens
  • Open-air & shaded
  • Parental supervision required
