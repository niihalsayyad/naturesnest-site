Resort Policies

Good to know before you arrive

To ensure a comfortable, safe and memorable stay for all our guests, please review our resort policies.

1. Cancellation Policy

  • We offer flexible cancellation options.
  • Guests are requested to inform resort management as early as possible about cancellations or changes to their booking.
  • Refunds and applicable cancellation charges will be handled according to the booking terms confirmed at the time of reservation.
  • Please contact us directly for cancellation assistance.

2. Pet Policy

  • Pets are permitted only with prior approval from resort management.
  • Guests must inform the resort about pets before arrival.
  • Pet owners are responsible for their pets' behaviour, cleanliness and safety.
  • Pets must not disturb other guests.

3. Children Policy

  • Children are welcome at Nature's Nest Exotic Resort.
  • Children must be accompanied and supervised by their parents or guardians.
  • Parents and guardians are responsible for children's safety throughout the property.

4. ID Proof Policy

  • Valid government-issued photo identification is mandatory for all adult guests during check-in.
  • Accepted identification may include Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving Licence or Voter ID.
  • All guests must provide accurate booking and identification details.
  • The resort reserves the right to verify guest identification during check-in.
